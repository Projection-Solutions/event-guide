Proposed Refined Training Plan — 4E Training Schedule

Target: Brand New Enlisted & CNTP nurses (top priority). Training is open to current staff, but student:instructor ratio is 6:1. If we anticipate more than 6 students, another instructor will be added.

Duration: 11 weeks (2.75 months). These include MEDSURG/PCC (8 weeks) & Trauma ICTLs (3 weeks). This will not change our Unit Orientation (MEDSURG) as this portion is 8 weeks long. Week 9–11 is trauma/ICTLs sustainment. Skills will be repeated every 15 weeks. Given 4 weeks break in between for makeup and rest.

Note: We will involve more nursing staff to teach on 2nd rotation and on. This will allow for us to identify more nurses motivated to teach and to train other staff.  |  Base Location: Sim Center South
